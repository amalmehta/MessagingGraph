The short version

Messaging Graph analyses your messages, including what other people wrote to you, on your Mac only. We never upload them, and we never see them. Not the messages, not your contacts, not statistics about either. There is no account and no server that holds your data.

What the app reads, and where it stays

When you choose to import, the app reads your Messages history (macOS's chat.db) and the names in your Contacts, and, if you choose to, your WhatsApp history. These contain words written by other people — the friends, family and others you talk to — as well as your own. We treat their words with the same care as yours:

  • Everything is read and analysed on your Mac. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.
  • The app keeps one encrypted copy of what it imported, in your user Library on your Mac. The key that decrypts it is kept in your macOS login Keychain, not beside the data.
  • Phone numbers and email addresses are replaced by keyed hashes inside the app, so they do not appear in its links, settings or logs.
  • You can delete the imported copy and its key at any time with the app's “wipe” command. Removing the app's Full Disk Access stops it reading your messages; what it already imported stays until you delete it.

The drafts the app suggests are written on your Mac. It never sends a message for you; you copy a draft into Messages yourself if you want to.

What goes online

The app makes exactly two kinds of network request. Neither contains anything from your messages or contacts, and the app enforces that in code: each request may only go to one address and carry a fixed, short list of fields. The app's Privacy screen lists both, and keeps a log of every attempt.

  • Licence check — only once you buy. When you activate a key, and then about once a week, the app sends your licence key, the activation id our payment provider gave it, and a random name made up on your Mac to Lemon Squeezy's licence service (api.lemonsqueezy.com). This is how a key is limited to your own Macs and how a refunded key stops working. The trial makes no licence requests at all.
  • Update check — at most once a week, the app downloads one small file from this website saying what the newest version is. The request contains nothing about you. Like any web request it reveals your IP address to the web host. You can switch this off in the app's Privacy screen.
