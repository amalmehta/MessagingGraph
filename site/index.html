Questions

Why does it ask for Full Disk Access?

macOS keeps your Messages history behind Full Disk Access, so no app can read it until you allow it. Messaging Graph walks you through switching it on the first time it opens. It only reads your messages while it imports them, and you can switch the permission back off afterwards — what it imported stays.

Does anything get uploaded?

No. Not your messages, not your contacts, not statistics about them. The only things that go online are the licence check and the weekly update check, and the app shows you both, in full, under Privacy.

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing disappears. Everything you imported stays readable. Importing new messages waits until you enter a licence key.

How many Macs can I use it on?

Your own Macs, up to two at a time. You can move a licence from one Mac to another from the app's Privacy screen.

Why isn't it on the Mac App Store?

App Store apps live in a sandbox that can't be given access to your Messages history, even with your permission. So it's sold here, signed by its developer and checked by Apple, the way many Mac apps are.

Does it work with WhatsApp?

Yes, if you use WhatsApp's Mac app. It's imported separately and shown alongside your Messages history.
